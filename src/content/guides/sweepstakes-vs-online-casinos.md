On the surface, sweepstakes casinos and real-money online casinos look nearly identical — the
same kinds of slots, similar table games, similar-looking sites. The real difference is legal and
financial, not visual.

## How you play

At a real-money online casino, you deposit cash directly and wager it. At a sweepstakes casino,
you play with **Gold Coins** (no cash value, for fun only) and separately-earned **Sweeps Coins**
(redeemable for prizes). You don't wager cash directly in either currency — see our
[how sweepstakes casinos work](/guides/how-sweepstakes-casinos-work/) guide for the full model.

## Where each is legal

Real-money online casino gambling is only legal in a small number of states that have
specifically regulated and licensed it. Sweepstakes casinos operate under a different legal
framework — promotional sweepstakes law — which is why they're available in many more states,
though a growing number have moved to restrict the model too. Check our
[legal-by-state guide](/casinos/legal-by-state/) for what we've found per brand.

## Age and identity requirements

Both models require you to be of legal age (18+, or 21+ in some states) and to verify your
identity before redeeming any prize — that part isn't really different.

## What you actually win

At a real-money casino, your winnings are just... money, deposited back to your account. At a
sweepstakes casino, Sweeps Coins convert to cash or gift cards at redemption, generally at a
fixed rate (often 1 Sweeps Coin = $1), subject to minimum-balance and playthrough requirements
that vary by operator.

## Which one should you use?

If real-money online casino gambling is legal and available where you live, that's a more
direct, traditional option. If it isn't — which is the case in most states — sweepstakes casinos
are the legal alternative that's actually available to you, with a comparable range of games.
