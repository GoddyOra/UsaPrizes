Chumba and Pulsz are two of the most recognizable names in the sweepstakes-casino space, and
they're often the first two brands people compare before signing up anywhere. Both run on the
same basic model — free Gold Coins for casual play, separately awarded Sweeps Coins that are
redeemable for real prizes — but the details underneath that shared structure are different
enough to matter. Here's how they actually stack up.

## Quick verdict

If you want the short version: Chumba has the longer track record, the bigger slot library, and
a faster top-end redemption method, which is why it edges out Pulsz in our
[overall rankings](/casinos/best-sweepstakes-casinos/) (4.3 vs. 4.0). Pulsz counters with a
simpler structure and frequently circulating promo codes that can make its real-world welcome
offer more competitive than the headline numbers suggest. Neither is a bad choice — which one
suits you better comes down to what you're optimizing for.

## Welcome bonus: bigger on paper at Chumba, but check Pulsz's active codes first

Chumba's headline welcome bonus is substantially larger than Pulsz's baseline no-code offer —
2,000,000 Gold Coins plus 2 Sweeps Coins, credited automatically with no purchase required. Pulsz
starts smaller by comparison (5,000 Gold Coins + 2.3 Sweeps Coins), but Pulsz promo codes for a
larger bundle circulate more often than Chumba's do, so the real gap between the two at the
moment you actually sign up can be smaller than the base numbers suggest. Neither requires a
purchase for its entry-level bonus — see our
[no-deposit bonus guide](/guides/sweepstakes-casino-no-deposit-bonuses/) for how to evaluate an
offer before claiming it, since headline coin counts don't always translate to the same real
value.

Gold Coins have no cash value at either brand — they're free-play currency, not the prize-
eligible one. The number that actually matters for both is the Sweeps Coins portion of the
bonus, since that's what's redeemable.

## Game selection: Chumba's library is larger, and it leans harder into bingo variants

Chumba is the more established of the two, and it shows in the size of its catalog — a large,
well-reviewed slot library that's grown over a longer operating history, plus bingo-style games
(including 75-ball, 90-ball, and Bingo Blast formats) that aren't really part of Pulsz's lineup.
If bingo variants specifically are something you're looking for, that's a real point in Chumba's
favor.

Pulsz keeps things more slots-focused. Its catalog is smaller than Chumba's, which is consistent
with its overall positioning — a simpler, more streamlined experience rather than the biggest
possible library. Neither brand publishes exact game counts we could independently verify, so
treat "larger" as a relative, not absolute, comparison.

## Redemption speed: Chumba's fastest option beats Pulsz's, but Pulsz gives you more paths

This is where the two diverge the most. Chumba's fastest redemption method — its prepaid
Mastercard option — is close to instant, meaningfully faster than anything Pulsz currently
offers. Chumba's other methods trail behind that: gift cards process in about 24 hours, Skrill
and Trustly average around 2 business days, and standard bank transfers can take 3–7 business
days.

Pulsz doesn't have an equivalent near-instant option. Its redemptions typically process within
about 5 days, with bank-based methods (ACH/Trustly) sometimes running 1–10 business days. Where
Pulsz does better is redemption method variety — ACH, Trustly, Skrill, or gift cards through
Prizeout — though "more options" doesn't necessarily mean "faster" here.

Both brands add an identity-verification step before your first redemption, and both require a
minimum balance and a completed playthrough first — Pulsz's minimum is explicitly 10 SC with a
1x playthrough. See our [full guide to redeeming sweepstakes winnings](/guides/how-to-redeem-sweepstakes-winnings/)
and our [KYC verification guide](/guides/sweepstakes-casino-kyc-verification/) for what that
first-redemption verification step actually involves at any operator.

## Which one should you actually pick?

**Pick Chumba if:** you want the biggest headline welcome bonus, the largest game library
(including bingo variants Pulsz doesn't offer), and the fastest possible redemption method for
when you do cash out.

**Pick Pulsz if:** you'd rather have a simpler, less cluttered experience, you're comfortable
checking for an active promo code before signing up, and near-instant redemption isn't a priority
for you.

**Or do both.** Since both offer free daily Gold Coin and Sweeps Coin bonuses with no purchase
required, a lot of players simply maintain accounts at both and let each one's free bonuses stack
up in parallel — there's no rule against it, and no cost to trying both.
