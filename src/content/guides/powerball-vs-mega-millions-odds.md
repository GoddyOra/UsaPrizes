Both are long-shot, life-changing jackpots, and the odds are close enough that the difference
rarely matters in practical terms — but there is a real difference, and it's worth knowing.

## Jackpot odds

| Game | Format | Jackpot odds | Ticket price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Powerball | Pick 5 from 1-69, plus 1 Powerball from 1-26 | 1 in 292,201,338 | $2 |
| Mega Millions | Pick 5 from 1-70, plus 1 Mega Ball from 1-24 | 1 in 290,472,336 | $5 |

Mega Millions changed its format in April 2025 — the ticket price rose to $5 and the Mega Ball
pool shrank, which slightly improved the jackpot odds compared to the old $2 format. If you see
older odds figures for Mega Millions elsewhere, they likely predate that change.

The two games' jackpot odds are close enough (within about 0.6%) that the difference isn't really
a practical factor in deciding which to play — both are extremely long-shot bets.

## Odds of winning *any* prize

Jackpot odds get the headlines, but most players who win, win a smaller fixed prize. Overall odds
of winning something (any tier) are roughly similar between the two games — in the neighborhood
of 1 in 24 to 1 in 25 for either game, though the exact figure shifts slightly with each game's
specific prize structure.

## Why the ticket price matters

Mega Millions costs $5 per play versus Powerball's $2, which is arguably the bigger practical
difference between the two games — not the odds. At more than double the price, Mega Millions
needs to offer meaningfully better value elsewhere (its April 2025 changes also increased some
non-jackpot prizes) to be worth it purely on a cost basis.

## Bottom line

Neither game has a meaningfully "better" chance at the jackpot — the odds are close enough not to
be the deciding factor. The real differences are ticket price ($2 vs. $5) and drawing schedule
(Powerball: Monday/Wednesday/Saturday; Mega Millions: Tuesday/Friday). Check
[current Powerball](/lottery/powerball/) and [Mega Millions](/lottery/mega-millions/) results and
jackpot sizes on our results pages, updated automatically after every drawing.
