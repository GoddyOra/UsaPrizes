A Sweeps Coin (often shortened to "SC," or given a different branded name depending on the
operator — Fortune Coins, WOW Coins, and so on) is the prize-eligible currency at a sweepstakes
casino. It's the one that actually turns into real money or a gift card.

## Sweeps Coins vs. Gold Coins

Every sweepstakes casino runs two currencies side by side:

- **Gold Coins** (or the operator's equivalent) are play-money only. You can buy them, and
  they're often given away free too, but they never convert to a prize — they're just there so
  you can play the games.
- **Sweeps Coins** are the one that matters for winning something real. You play through them in
  the same games as Gold Coins, and any Sweeps Coins you accumulate can eventually be redeemed.

See our [how sweepstakes casinos work](/guides/how-sweepstakes-casinos-work/) guide for the full
model and why this two-currency structure is what makes the whole thing legal.

## How you get Sweeps Coins

By law, every sweepstakes operator has to offer a way to get Sweeps Coins for free — a daily
login bonus, a mail-in request, or similar (the specific "alternative method of entry" varies by
operator). Sweeps Coins are also commonly bundled as a free bonus with paid Gold Coin purchases,
which is fine as long as the free route genuinely exists too.

## Turning Sweeps Coins into a real prize

Most operators require:

- A minimum Sweeps Coin balance before you can request redemption.
- A playthrough requirement (you generally need to have played through the coins, not just
  collected them).
- Identity verification, typically on your first redemption.

Once those are met, Sweeps Coins usually redeem at a fixed rate — often 1 SC = $1, though this
varies by operator — for cash or a gift card. Redemption speed itself varies a lot brand to
brand: see individual reviews, like [Chumba](/casinos/reviews/chumba/) or
[LuckyLand](/casinos/reviews/luckyland/), for specifics.

## The bottom line

Sweeps Coins are what make sweepstakes casinos different from playing a free-to-play game with
no prize at the end — they're the actual redeemable currency, earned rather than purchased. Gold
Coins get you into the games; Sweeps Coins are what you're really playing for.
