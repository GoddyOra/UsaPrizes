Checking your Powerball numbers takes about ten seconds once you know where to look — here's the
quick version, plus what happens next if you actually matched.

## Step 1: Get the official winning numbers

Powerball drawings happen every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday night. Our
[Powerball results page](/lottery/powerball/) updates automatically after each drawing with the
five main numbers, the Powerball number, and the next drawing date.

## Step 2: Compare your ticket

Powerball prizes are based on how many of your numbers match, and whether one of them is the red
Powerball:

| Match | Prize |
|---|---|
| 5 numbers + Powerball | Jackpot |
| 5 numbers | $1,000,000 |
| 4 numbers + Powerball | $50,000 |
| 4 numbers | $100 |
| 3 numbers + Powerball | $100 |
| 3 numbers | $7 |
| 2 numbers + Powerball | $7 |
| 1 number + Powerball | $4 |
| Powerball only | $4 |

Prize amounts below the jackpot are fixed, though some can increase further if you added the
Power Play multiplier option. This table reflects the standard prize structure; always confirm
the exact payout for a specific drawing with your official state lottery.

## Step 3: Verify through an official source

Our results page is sourced from New York's public lottery data feed and is meant for quick,
informational reference — but if you have a winning ticket, always verify directly through your
state lottery's official website or retailer terminal before making any decisions, and never rely
solely on a third-party site to confirm a claim.

## If you actually won

Don't sign the back of the ticket yet — read your state lottery's specific claiming instructions
first, since requirements vary by state and by prize size. For any meaningful prize, most experts
recommend securing the physical ticket immediately, taking a photo of both sides, and consulting
a financial advisor before making the drawing amount public or making any big decisions. For an
idea of what a jackpot win might mean at tax time, see our
[lottery winnings tax guide](/guides/how-lottery-winnings-are-taxed/).

## What if nobody wins?

If nobody matches all six numbers, the jackpot rolls over and grows for the next drawing — see
our [guide to what happens when nobody wins the jackpot](/guides/what-happens-if-no-one-wins-the-jackpot/)
for how that process works.
