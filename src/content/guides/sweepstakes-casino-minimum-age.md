Short answer: usually 18, sometimes 21 — and unlike real-money casino gambling, the age minimum
for sweepstakes casinos isn't primarily set by state law. It's set by each operator.

## Why this is different from a state-mandated gambling age

Real-money casino gambling age limits come from state gaming law, and they're consistent within
a state regardless of which casino you're at. Sweepstakes casinos operate under promotional
sweepstakes law instead (see our
[how sweepstakes casinos work](/guides/how-sweepstakes-casinos-work/) guide for why), and most
states don't set a specific sweepstakes-only age minimum. That leaves individual operators to set
their own policy — most land on 18+, but a growing number set 21+ voluntarily, often to mirror
real-money casino norms or to reduce their own regulatory risk in stricter states.

## What this means practically

Because it's operator-by-operator rather than a single state rule, you can't assume every
sweepstakes casino uses the same age minimum even within the same state. Always check the
specific operator's terms before signing up — it's usually stated clearly during registration,
and often mentioned on the operator's own FAQ or terms page.

## It's separate from state availability

Age minimum and state availability are two different gates. An operator might be available in
your state but still require you to be 21 rather than 18, or vice versa. Our
[legal-by-state guide](/casinos/legal-by-state/) tracks *availability* per brand; age minimum is
a separate thing to check directly with whichever operator you're considering.

## The bottom line

If you're 21 or older, age generally won't be a blocker anywhere. If you're 18-20, you'll have
access to most sweepstakes casinos but not all of them — check the specific operator's stated
minimum before you sign up, rather than assuming 18+ applies universally.
