Redeeming Sweeps Coins for a real prize follows roughly the same steps at every sweepstakes
casino, even though the exact numbers and timing vary quite a bit by brand.

## The general process

1. **Accumulate Sweeps Coins.** These come from free daily bonuses, occasional promotions, or as
   a bundled gift with a Gold Coin purchase — see our [what is a Sweeps Coin](/guides/what-is-a-sweeps-coin/)
   guide if you're not familiar with the two-currency model.
2. **Meet the minimum balance.** Most operators won't let you redeem until you've hit a minimum
   Sweeps Coin balance — this varies significantly, from around 10 SC at some operators up to 50
   SC or more at others.
3. **Clear the playthrough requirement.** You generally need to have played through your Sweeps
   Coins at least once (a "1x playthrough" is common) rather than just holding them.
4. **Verify your identity.** Expect to submit a photo ID the first time you redeem — this is
   standard across the industry and adds a delay on your very first request specifically.
5. **Choose a redemption method.** Options and speed vary a lot by operator — see below.
6. **Wait for processing.** Anywhere from near-instant to several business days, again depending
   on the operator and method chosen.

## What actually determines the wait

The single biggest factor is the redemption method, not the operator's overall reliability.
Prepaid card and cryptocurrency redemptions tend to be fastest — sometimes near-instant. Digital
wallet options like Skrill or Trustly are usually a middle ground, often a couple of business
days. Traditional bank transfers are typically the slowest, sometimes taking the better part of a
week. First-time redemptions are also slower everywhere because of the identity-verification
step.

If redemption speed matters more to you than anything else, prioritize operators offering
crypto or prepaid-card redemption over ones relying solely on bank transfer — the difference can
be several business days.
